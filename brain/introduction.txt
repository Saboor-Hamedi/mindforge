================================================================================
MINDFORGE: MINIMALIST KEYBOARD NOTEBOOK
================================================================================

MindForge is a lightweight, distraction-free notebook engineered for capturing
your thoughts, code, and ideas with fluid responsiveness and zero friction.

All notes are stored locally and securely on your computer in an embedded
SQLite database. Your thoughts and files remain 100% private and yours.


1. CORE PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Instant & Offline     Zero loading spinners, no cloud sync lag, no accounts.
  * Private by Default    Notes, settings, and activity logs stay on your device.
  * Silky Smooth Render   High refresh-rate UI with monospace font ligatures.
  * Keyboard Ergonomics   Navigate, search, edit, and organize without a mouse.
  * Fully Customizable    Custom keybindings, color themes, carets, and sounds.


2. QUICK START GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [Ctrl+N]          Create a new blank note and start typing immediately.
  [Ctrl+S]          Manual note save (MindForge also auto-saves continuously).
  [Ctrl+Shift+X]    Toggle checklist checkbox between [ ] and [x].
  [Ctrl+E]          Toggle between Inline Live Markdown and Raw Plaintext mode.
  [Ctrl+J]          Open the embedded interactive terminal dock (:term).
  [Ctrl+Shift+I]    Open the DeepSeek AI Assistant right pane.
  [Ctrl+.]          Enter distraction-free Zen focus mode (:zen).
  [Ctrl+B]          Toggle the notes sidebar library.
  [Ctrl+P]          Fast fuzzy search across all notes by title or content.
  [Ctrl+,]          Open preferences (themes, carets, sounds, keys, AI).
